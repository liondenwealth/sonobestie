Sono Bestie Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Your information

Sono Bestie does not require an account. The content you create in the app, such as notes, progress and settings, is stored on your device. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Optional features

Some optional features, such as local weather and job search, may use your approximate location or your search terms to work. These are shared with outside service providers only when you use those features, and they handle that information under their own privacy policies. You can turn off location access at any time in your device settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions, if you choose to buy one, are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Sono Bestie is intended for adults and students in the sonography field and is not directed to children under 13.

Professional use

Sono Bestie is a reference and everyday tool for sonography professionals and students. It supports, and does not replace, professional judgment and your department's protocols. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: sonobestie@gmail.com.
